Admission

Admission is open for 2026-27. Apply in four steps.

  1. Fill the enquiry form below with your course choice.

Common questions

When do admissions close?

Admissions for 2026-27 are open now. Seats are filled in order of applications, so apply early.

Can I pay fees in installments?

Yes. You can pay yearly or in two installments each year.

Is hostel available?

Yes. Hostel with meals costs ₹70,000 per year and is optional.

Which documents do I need?

Previous mark sheets, an ID proof and recent passport-size photographs.
